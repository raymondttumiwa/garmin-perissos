Pipeline A: Hybrid Cloud & Garmin Sync

Syncs both manual devotions & Garmin biometrics via Supabase

How Hybrid Sync Works Across Your Devices:

1. Your GitHub Action runs garmin_sync.py hourly to update watch metrics (steps, sleep_hours, stress_avg) in garmin_daily_metrics.

2. When you check off a Morning Devotion or manual habit on your phone or laptop, it saves into the manual_logs JSONB column for that date so both worlds stay in sync!
